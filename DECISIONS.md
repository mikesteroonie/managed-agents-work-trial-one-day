# Design notes

Keep brief notes here as the product takes shape.

## Approach

What customer need did you prioritize? Which product and technical choices mattered most?

## Changes

What did you learn from questions, testing, or watching someone use the product? What did you change?

## Running the product

Document the commands to start, migrate, and check the application.

## Remaining work

What are the current limitations, and what would you address next?
